Discoveries
- Score checks reliably clarify HP/status when other reports are unclear.
- Clearing menu interruptions is the first recovery step before anything else.
- Gear pickup can fail from bad container/command syntax; exact visible names matter.

Mistakes
- Repeating ineffective NPC asking at 1 HP instead of recovering or fixing gear.
- Pressing on with a stalled approach when no stabilization or leveling progress was being made.
- Relying on unavailable or nonfunctional actions instead of changing tactics.
- Repeated pickup attempts with misparsed or “not a container” results when no item was gained.

Strategies
- Use consider before committing to combat and keep resource use cautious.
- Leave a stalled fight cleanly if progress stops or the target is gone.
- Check score when status reports are suspicious or fail to clarify HP.
- Clear menu interruptions immediately, then reassess status before acting.
- Verify item names and inventory carefully before trying pickup/equipment actions.
