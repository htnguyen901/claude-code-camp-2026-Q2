Discoveries
- Being broke and empty-handed blocks paid recovery; always check gold/inventory first.
- Free water is available in Temple Square; hunger may still need a different free or charitable source.
- The bartender/inn lead is approachable by inquiry, but not yet a guaranteed solution.
- The Poor Alley beggar lead is exhausted; do not revisit it expecting more immediate help.

Mistakes
- Pressing on while hurt or hungry remains unsafe.
- Repeating dead-end leads or vague poking wastes turns; use direct look/examine and move on when a lead is spent.
- Assuming paid services will solve recovery without confirming resources first.

Strategies
- Check status, gold, and inventory before committing to recovery or fights.
- Prefer free food, charity, or safe income over paid services when broke.
- Use direct look/examine and clear leads; replan immediately when a blocker is confirmed.
- Continue prioritizing survival and recovery before leveling.

Open threads
- Find a free food, charity, or safe way to earn gold.
- Restore HP and hunger so leveling to 20 becomes possible.
