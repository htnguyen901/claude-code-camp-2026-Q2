# World Map & Locations

## The Bakery
**Description:** You are standing inside the small bakery. A sweet scent of danish and fine bread fills the room. The bread and Danish are arranged in fine order on the shelves, and seem to be of the finest quality. A small sign is on the counter.

**Location:** North of Main Street in the City of Midgaard

**NPCs:** The baker (looks at you calmly, wiping flour from his face)

**Exits:** South (to Main Street)

**Menu Items:**
1. **Danish** - Sweet pastries, arranged neatly on shelves
2. **Bread** - Fine quality bread, freshly baked

**Notes:** The bakery appears to sell freshly baked goods. The baker can assist with purchases.

## City Layout

### Temple Area
- **The Temple of Midgaard** - Southern end with ATM
  - North → By The Temple Altar
  - South → Temple Square/Courtyard
  - East → The Midgaard Donation Room  
  - West → The Reading Room
  - Down → (leads somewhere)

- **By The Temple Altar** - Northern end with statue of Odin
  - North → Behind The Temple Altar (countryside path)
  - South → The Temple of Midgaard

- **Behind The Temple Altar** - Dirt path towards Dragonhelm Mountains
  - North → Countryside (continues north)
  - South → By The Temple Altar

### City Streets
- **Main Street** - Through City of Midgaard
  - North → The Bakery
  - South → The Armory
  - East → Market Square
  - West → (unexplored)

- **The Armory** - South of Main Street
  - North → Main Street
  - Features: Peacekeeper, Armorer

### Shops
- **The Reading Room** - West of Temple
  - East → The Temple of Midgaard
  - Features: Saleswoman with gadgets/gizmos
  - Item: Teleporter device

- **The Midgaard Donation Room** - East of Temple  
  - West → The Temple of Midgaard
  - Features: Donation NPC

- **The Bakery** - North of Main Street
  - South → Main Street
  - Menu: Danish, Bread

### Market Areas
- **Market Square** - East of Main Street (unexplored)
